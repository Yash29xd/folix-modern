Last updated:

Folix - A FloppersBlop Product renders and processes PDFs locally in your browser using client-side libraries. Output may differ from other viewers, especially for unusual fonts, transparency effects, forms, or damaged files.

  • Irreversible Permanent Redaction: Sanitization permanently burns solid #000000 blackouts into flattened page bitmaps and irreversibly deletes underlying text, fonts, and hidden layers. Once saved, redacted data cannot be unmasked or recovered. Always retain an unredacted master backup.
  • No Password Recovery: Password encryption executes 100% on-device with no server escrow. FloppersBlop cannot recover lost or forgotten Open or Master passwords under any circumstances.
  • Client-Side Compression & Word Studio: PDF compression rasterizes pages at your chosen DPI and JPEG quality, which trades vector sharpness for smaller file size. Word Studio drafts live solely in temporary browser memory until exported or opened in the viewer.
  • Verification: Do not rely on Folix - A FloppersBlop Product as the sole check for legal, financial, medical, or safety-critical documents. Always verify against the original source.
  • Search & Content: Text search depends on the PDF containing real text; scanned pages require running on-device OCR or Snip OCR. We are not responsible for the content of PDFs you open.
