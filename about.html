About Folix

Last updated:

Folix is a fast, lightweight, privacy-first PDF viewer and editor that runs entirely in your browser. It is a product of .

What it does

  • Continuous scrolling, zoom, fit-width, fit-height, and smooth page navigation
  • On-device Optical Character Recognition (OCR) with Tesseract.js for scanned documents
  • Searchable PDF export with embedded invisible text layers
  • Text selection, copy, and in-document search with context highlights
  • Password-protected PDFs, outline tree, and lazy page thumbnails
  • Vector annotations studio: pen, highlighter, text highlights, sticky notes, undo/redo, and lossless PDF export
  • Digital signatures (draw or type) and inline textboxes
  • Document translation (on-device Chromium AI + optional MyMemory fallback) with page overlays
  • Interactive AcroForm form filling, clickable PDF hyperlinks, and Text-to-Speech (TTS) read aloud
  • Page tools: drag-and-drop reordering, rotation, deletion, page extraction, and PDF merging
  • Recent files dashboard, annotation manager, and single-page PNG export
  • Works 100% offline as an installable Progressive Web App (PWA)
  • Tri-theme engine (Light, Sepia, Dark mode) plus Spotlight night reading mode

How it works

Folix combines Mozilla's PDF.js rendering pipeline, pdf-lib for lossless document manipulation, and WebAssembly Tesseract.js for optical character recognition. All rendering, vector calculations, and OCR execute locally inside your browser sandbox—zero documents ever leave your machine.
